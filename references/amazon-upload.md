# Using the versions on Amazon

## Sponsored Brands video

1. One **ad group per keyword** (or per tight group of keywords that share a scene).
2. Target that ad group at its keyword on exact match, so the shopper who sees the
   "hot dog" opening is the shopper who typed "hot dog".
3. Upload that keyword's version as the ad group's video.
4. Keep the original video running on the same keyword. It is the control. Amazon reviews
   every new video before it runs, like any ad.
5. After 1 to 2 weeks compare click-through rate, per keyword, against the original on the
   same keyword. Keep the winners, cut the rest.

## What Amazon asks of the file

Checked against Amazon's Sponsored Brands video spec page (November 2026). `inspect`
warns when the source video already breaks one of these; the versions keep whatever the
source had.

- 6 to 45 seconds
- 1280x720, 1920x1080 or 3840x2160, no black bars
- 23.976 to 30 frames per second
- .mp4 or .mov, H.264 or H.265, up to 500 MB
- Audio: AAC, PCM or MP3

The video starts playing on its own when half of it is on screen, **without sound**. The
shopper turns the sound on. So the first picture and the words on it do the work, which
is the whole reason to match them to the search.

## Anywhere else

The same files work wherever an ad is aimed at a search term or an audience: one version
per term, per audience, per season.
